# 💻 Society of Information Technology Students (SITS)

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of the **Society of Information Technology Students (SITS)** at **Southern Leyte State University - Hinunangan Campus**! 🚀

---

### 📌 About Us
We are the official student organization representing the **Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT)** students at SLSU - Hinunangan Campus. Our community is dedicated to fostering technical excellence, innovation, collaboration, and continuous learning among IT enthusiasts.

### 🎯 Our Mission
* To empower BSIT students through hands-on technical experience and open-source contributions.
* To collaborate on academic, community-driven, and open-source projects.
* To bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world technology solutions.
